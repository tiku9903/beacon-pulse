# Electrical Safety

## Immediate safety advice
If a light or fan is sparking, smoking, buzzing loudly, smells of burning, or is hot to the touch, switch it off at the wall and at the circuit breaker (switchboard) immediately and do not use it again. If there is fire or smoke, leave the area and call 000.

## Installation must be done by a licensed electrician
In Australia, hardwired light fittings and ceiling fans must be installed by a licensed electrician. Never attempt to wire a fitting yourself. Plug-in lamps and smart bulbs can be installed by the customer.

## Product recalls
Current product recalls are listed on the ACCC Product Safety Australia website. If you believe your product is affected, stop using it and contact us immediately.
