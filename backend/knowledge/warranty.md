# Warranty

## Standard warranty
Beacon Lighting products carry a 12-month manufacturer's warranty from the date of purchase unless stated otherwise on the product page. This is in addition to your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

## Ceiling fan warranty
DC ceiling fan motors carry a 5-year motor warranty and 12 months on parts. AC ceiling fan motors carry a 3-year motor warranty and 12 months on parts. Fans must be installed by a licensed electrician for the warranty to apply; please keep the installer's certificate of compliance.

## LED and smart lighting warranty
Integrated LED fittings and downlights carry a 3-year warranty. Smart bulbs and LED strip kits carry a 2-year warranty. Globes are covered for 12 months.

## How to make a warranty claim
Provide your order number or receipt, the product name, a description of the fault, and photos or a short video. For hardwired fittings, include the electrician's certificate of compliance. Approved claims are resolved by repair, replacement or refund.
