# Stores & Trading Hours

## Store trading hours
Most Beacon Lighting stores are open Monday to Friday 9am–5:30pm, Saturday 9am–5pm and Sunday 10am–4pm. Hours vary by location and on public holidays; check the store finder on our website for your local store.

## Price match
We will match a competitor's current advertised price on an identical in-stock item. Trade-only, clearance and marketplace prices are excluded.
