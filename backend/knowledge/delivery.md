# Delivery & Click and Collect

## Delivery times
Metro deliveries usually arrive in 2–5 business days and regional deliveries in 4–10 business days after dispatch. You will receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

## Click and Collect
Click and Collect orders are usually ready within 2 hours during store trading hours when the item is in stock at the selected store. Bring your order confirmation and photo ID. Orders are held for 7 days.

## Tracking an order
Use the tracking link in your dispatch email, or contact us with your order number (it starts with BL) and we will send the latest status.
