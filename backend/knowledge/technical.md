# Technical Support

## Smart bulb will not connect to Wi-Fi
Smart bulbs connect to 2.4GHz Wi-Fi only. Reset the bulb by switching it off and on five times (about one second each) until it flashes, then add it again in the app while your phone is on the same 2.4GHz network.

## LED downlight flickering
Flickering is usually caused by an incompatible dimmer or by mixing LED and halogen fittings on one circuit. Check that the dimmer is listed as compatible with the downlight. If flickering continues on a non-dimmed circuit, have an electrician check the driver and connections.

## Ceiling fan wobbling or noisy
Make sure all blade screws are tight and the blades are seated evenly; use the balancing kit supplied in the box. A humming noise on AC fans can come from some speed controllers. Any repair to the mounting bracket or wiring must be done by a licensed electrician.

## Ceiling fan remote not working
Replace the remote battery, then re-pair the remote: switch the fan off at the wall for 10 seconds, switch it back on and hold the pairing button on the remote within 30 seconds.
