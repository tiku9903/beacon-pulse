# Returns & Refunds

## Change-of-mind returns
Unused items in original, undamaged packaging can be returned within 30 days of purchase (or of delivery for online orders) for a refund or exchange. Proof of purchase is required. Globes, custom-cut LED strip and clearance items are not eligible for change-of-mind returns.

## How to return an online order
Return the item to any Beacon Lighting store with your order number, or request a prepaid return label from our support team. Once the item is received and inspected, the refund is issued to the original payment method.

## Refund processing time
Approved refunds are processed within 2 business days. Banks usually take a further 3–5 business days to show the funds. Refunds over the automatic limit are reviewed by a team member before release.

## Faulty or damaged on arrival
If an item arrives damaged or faulty, contact us within 7 days with photos of the item and packaging. We will arrange a replacement or refund at no cost. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law are not affected by these policies.
