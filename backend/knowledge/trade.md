# Beacon Trade

## Trade accounts
Beacon Trade accounts are available to licensed electricians, builders, designers and developers. Benefits include trade pricing, 30-day account terms (subject to approval), a dedicated account manager for Gold and Platinum members, and priority support.

## Bulk and project orders
For bulk or project orders, our trade desk can provide quotes, reserve stock across stores, and schedule staged deliveries to site. Quotes are usually returned within one business day.
